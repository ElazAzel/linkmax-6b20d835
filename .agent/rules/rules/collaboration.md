# Collaboration Rules

## Handoff Format

Every delegated task or role transition includes:

1. Objective and expected behavior.
2. In-scope files, interfaces, and constraints.
3. Acceptance criteria.
4. Required verification and any known risk.

Use the active task/conversation for progress. Do not assume persistent files such as `task.md`, `implementation_plan.md`, or `walkthrough.md` exist.

## Shared Rules

- Read current code and `docs/README.md` before acting on historical plans or audits.
- Do not overwrite, revert, or clean unrelated local changes.
- Keep one owner for a changed contract at a time: schema/RLS, API, UI behavior, or CI workflow.
- Surface blockers with evidence, proposed options, and their impact.
- The final owner confirms verification evidence and updates current documentation.
