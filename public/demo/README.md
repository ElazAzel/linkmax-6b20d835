# Example photography

These bundled WebP photographs are illustrative stock media for example pages and
starter templates. The depicted people are not represented as LinkMAX customers.
Do not use their portraits as a new user's identity or imply verification.

Original image URLs and downloaded byte sizes are recorded in `sources.json`.
Source: Unsplash, https://unsplash.com/license. Images are sized for their actual
display use and served locally so page examples do not depend on a third-party
image request at runtime.
